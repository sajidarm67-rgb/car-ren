Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we get asked the most. If yours is not here, feel free to contact us.

How old do I need to be to rent a car?

You must be at least 21 years old and hold a valid driving licence.

What documents do I need at pick-up?

A valid driving licence and a valid ID such as your CNIC or passport. If paying by card, please bring the card as well.

Is there a security deposit?

Yes, a refundable security deposit may be required depending on the car category. It is returned after the car is returned in good condition.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Cancellations made at least 24 hours before pick-up are free. Later cancellations may be charged one day's rental.

Are the prices in PKR?

Yes, all prices on our website are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR) per day.

Is fuel included in the rental price?

No. Cars are provided with a certain fuel level and should be returned at the same level. Any shortfall is charged at the end of the rental.

Can I take the car outside Karachi?

Yes, but please tell us in advance so we can adjust the insurance and inform you about any extra charges.

What happens if the car breaks down?

Call our 24/7 support line. We will arrange assistance or a replacement car as quickly as possible.

Do you offer rentals with a driver?

Yes. We offer self-drive and with-driver options. You can mention your preference in the booking notes.

Can I extend my rental period?

Usually yes, subject to availability. Contact us at least a day before your return date so we can update your booking.
