Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

At Rent-a-Car, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Your name, email address, and phone number when you fill out the booking form.
  • Your pick-up and return dates and the car you select.
  • Any special requests or notes you add to your reservation.

How we use your information

  • To confirm and manage your car rental booking.
  • To contact you about your reservation if needed.
  • To improve our website and services.

Sharing your information

We do not sell or trade your personal information. We only share details with our staff and trusted partners who help us complete your rental.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, but no website can guarantee complete security.

Cookies

This demo website does not use cookies. If we add them in the future, this policy will be updated.

Your rights

You can ask us to view, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please write to us at rentaCar.example.
