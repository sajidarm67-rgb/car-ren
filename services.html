Our Services

We offer more than just car rentals. Here is what you can expect when you rent with us.

Self-drive rentals

Take the wheel yourself. Choose any car from our fleet and drive it on your own schedule. Ideal for daily commutes, weekend trips, and business travel.

Rentals with driver

Prefer to relax? Book a car with an experienced driver who knows the city well. Perfect for airport pickups, weddings, and corporate events.

Long-term rentals

Need a car for a month or longer? We offer discounted long-term rates for corporate clients and individuals. Ask us about monthly packages.

Airport pick and drop

We can meet you at the airport with your car ready to go, or drop you off in time for your flight. Just mention it in your booking notes.

Wedding and event cars

Make your special day memorable with our premium cars. Decorations and a driver can be arranged on request.

Corporate packages

We work with companies to provide reliable transport for their staff and guests. Flexible billing and priority support included.

24/7 roadside assistance

If anything goes wrong on the road, our team is one call away. We help with flat tyres, battery issues, and towing.
